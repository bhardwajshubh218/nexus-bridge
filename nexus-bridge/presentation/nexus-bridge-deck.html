The solution

A bridge layer that links identities, without migrating anything

Nexus Registry

Resolves a legacy ID to its SES canonical ID only when exactly one mapping exists.

exact_keylinks
tax_id + legal_namelinks
bank_acct + tax_idlinks
name similarity 0.96never links alone

Identity stamping

Every transaction payload gets canonical IDs injected in flight. Legacy fields stay exactly as they were.

"txn_id": "TXN-…44810",
"source_mxm_id": "MXM-77329",
"amount": 12880.00,
"source_canonical_id": "SES-8842-ACME",
"destination_canonical_id": "SES-9001-BETA"

Safe exceptions

Missing, conflicting or ambiguous mappings are held for a person to decide. Similarity scores never decide anything.

one candidate entitypass
tax_id exact · 4421 vs 4422fail
settlement bank exactfail
MXM-99110 → review queuenot merged
Legacy systems stay unchanged. Fields are only added, never renamed or removed, and stamping can be switched off with a flag.
